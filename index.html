Hii,I am Dog

Hi! I am a **Dog**. 🐶 I am one of the most loyal and friendly pets that people keep at home. I love to **play, run, and spend time with my owner**. I enjoy going for walks and playing games like **fetch**. I am very protective, so I often **guard the house and keep my family safe**. I communicate by **barking, wagging my tail, and showing different body movements**. When I am happy, I wag my tail a lot and like to be petted. I need **food, exercise, and love** from my owner. Hii, I am Cat

Hi! I am a **Cat**. 🐱 I am a small, soft, and furry pet that many people love to keep at home. I like to **sleep a lot, play with toys, and sometimes sit on my owner's lap**. I may look quiet, but I am very playful and curious. I can also help my owner by **catching mice and small pests**. I keep myself **very clean** because I lick my fur to groom it. When I am happy, I **purr**, and when I want attention, I **meow**. Even though I like my **independence**, I also enjoy love and care from my owner. If you treat me kindly, I will become your **loving and loyal companion**. 🐾
